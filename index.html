What We Do

Escort services for every kind of load

From a single lead car on a routine wide load to a full superload convoy with height poles, route survey and utility coordination — we scale to the move.

Lead & Chase Escorts

Certified lead and chase vehicles with amber light bars, OVERSIZE LOAD signage, CB and two-way radio contact with your driver. We warn traffic, hold intersections and keep the load moving safely.

Details

High Pole / Height Pole

A high pole car runs ahead with the pole set just above your measured load height, clearing every overpass, signal, span and line before your load gets there. Required on most over-height permits.

Details

Route Surveys & Permits

We physically drive your route before the move — measuring clearances, turn radii, grades and construction zones — and flag problems while there's still time to reroute.

Night & Superload Moves

Permitted night runs, multi-car convoys, police escort coordination and utility line lifts. When the move needs more than one car, we build and run the whole escort package.

Why Wells Safety

Fewer surprises on the road

Most delays on an oversize move are avoidable — a low bridge nobody measured, a permit condition nobody read, an escort that never showed. We work to remove all three.

Certified & Insured

PEVO certified operators with ATSSA flagger, WITPAC and TIMS training. Commercial auto and general liability; certificates and cards sent on request.

Dispatch That Answers

A real person picks up, day or night. Same-day and next-morning coverage on most lanes, and a callback with a firm answer instead of a maybe.

Route Knowledge

We survey before we escort. Clearances, construction, weight-restricted spans and tight turns get caught on paper — not with your load stopped under a bridge.

Properly Equipped

Amber LED light bars, compliant OVERSIZE LOAD signs and flags, height poles, CB and handheld radios, cones, paddles, flags, fire extinguisher and full flagging kit in every vehicle.

Permit-Aware

We read the permit conditions with you — escort counts, travel windows, curfews, holiday restrictions — so nobody discovers a violation at a scale house.

Safety First, Always

Pre-trip inspections, documented communication protocols and a clean record. Our job is to protect your load, your driver and everybody else on that road.

Who We Are

Veteran owned. Badge tested.

Wells Safety is owned and operated by a U.S. Army veteran and retired police officer. Two careers built on the same thing this one needs: planning the route before you drive it, reading a road for what can go wrong, and staying calm when it does.

That background is not decoration. Years of traffic control and roadway incident management is exactly the skill set an escort driver needs when a load has to be stopped, a lane has to be held, or an intersection has to be cleared safely with traffic still moving.
