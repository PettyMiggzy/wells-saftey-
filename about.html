Our Story

Two careers in safety. Now one company.

Wells Safety is owned and operated by a U.S. Army veteran and retired police officer. Before this company there was a tour of service, and then years on patrol working crashes, closures and traffic control on live roadways — the exact conditions an oversize load moves through every single day.

That is not a marketing line. When a load has to be stopped short of a low span, when a lane has to be held while a 16-foot-wide trailer swings through an intersection, or when something goes wrong at mile marker 240 at two in the morning, the difference between a delay and an accident is whether the person in the escort vehicle has done it before under pressure. Ours has.

We built the rest of the company around the same standard. Every driver is directly employed, vetted and certified for the states on the route. Every survey is physically driven. Every booking gets a real confirmation from a real person, and a phone that gets answered.

How We Work

Three commitments

We show up early

Staged and radio-checked before your appointment time. If we are ever going to be late, you hear it from us first, not from your driver.

We tell you what we find

If a survey turns up a clearance that kills the route, you get that news immediately — even when it means the move gets postponed.

We say no when we should

If we cannot legally or safely cover a lane, we say so and help you find someone who can. A bad yes costs everybody more than an honest no.
