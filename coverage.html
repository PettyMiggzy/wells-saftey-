Reciprocity

Certification is state by state

There is no single national pilot car certification. Each state sets its own training, testing and vehicle requirements, and only some of them honor each other's cards.

Certification States

States like Utah, Washington, Colorado, Arizona, New Mexico, Florida, Oklahoma, Georgia, Minnesota, Virginia and New York require a specific escort certification card before you can legally run a load.

Reciprocal States

Several states accept another state's certification. We track which cards are honored where, and assign a driver whose credentials cover every state on your route.

Multi-State Routes

On long runs we either staff a driver certified end to end, or hand off at the state line to a vetted partner — arranged and scheduled by us, so you keep one point of contact.

Not On The List?

Ask anyway

We add states as our drivers certify in them, and we keep a partner network for the rest. Tell us the lane and we will tell you honestly whether we can cover it.
