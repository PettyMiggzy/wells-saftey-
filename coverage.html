Reciprocity

Certification is state by state

There is no single national pilot car certification. Each state sets its own training, testing and vehicle requirements, and only some of them honor each other's cards.

Certification States

States like Utah, Washington, Colorado, Arizona, New Mexico, Florida, Oklahoma, Georgia, Minnesota, Virginia and New York require a specific escort certification card before you can legally run a load. Our operators are PEVO and WITPAC certified.

Reciprocal States

Several states accept another state's certification. We track which cards are honored where, and assign a driver whose credentials cover every state on your route.

Multi-State Routes

On long runs we staff a driver certified end to end wherever we can, so the same escort stays with your load the whole way. Either way you keep one point of contact and one invoice.

Long Hauls Welcome

Tell us the lane

Cross-country runs are routine for us — we are set up to stay with a load from origin to delivery rather than handing it off at every state line. Send the route and we will confirm coverage and escort requirements for the whole run.
